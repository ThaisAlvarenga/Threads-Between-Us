This project was developed by [Thais Alvarenga](https://thaisalvarenga.com) in fullfillment of the project requirement for the Master's of Science in Digital Media at Georgia Institute of Technology. Dr. [Michael Nitsche](https://lmc.gatech.edu/people/person/michael-nitsche) served as primary advisor and chair of the committee formed by himself, Dr. Ryan Scheiding and Dr. Brian Magerko. Professor Lisa Marks from the Industrial Design department at Georgia Tech served as craft production advisor. 


Materials for the production of the project and for the deployment of the cultural probes were sponsored by the [Digital World and Image Group](https://dwig.lmc.gatech.edu/projects.html).
